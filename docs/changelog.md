## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-22

First release on PyPI.

- `qday scan`: TLS endpoints and source code in eight languages, risk levels and break years, `--fail-on` for CI.
- `qday cbom`: CycloneDX 1.6 cryptography bill of materials.
- `qday forecast`: published attack costs against IBM's quantum roadmap, two scenarios.
- `qday shor` and `qday grover`: the attacks on simulators or IBM quantum hardware.
- `qday pqc`: hybrid X25519 + ML-KEM-768 handshake.
- Hardware-calibrated forecast (`qday.forecast.calibrated`) from our runs on `ibm_fez`, `ibm_kingston` and
  `ibm_marrakesh`.
